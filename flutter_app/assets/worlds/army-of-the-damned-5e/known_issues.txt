Army of the Damned — port status (world-blueprint 1.0.0)

Ported from armyofthedamned.pdf (74 pages). `--check` is clean
(220 entities) and `audit_coverage.py` reports 99.7% — 6 sentences below the
threshold, all extraction artifacts, listed below.

Extraction notes (read before "fixing" anything)
- APPENDIX B's stat blocks are raster images: `pdftotext` recovers only the
  flavour paragraphs, no numbers. All 13 blocks (Ashmouth Hound, Charmbreaker
  Devil, Demonlord of Ashmouth, Fire Geist, Hellrider, Oglor, Rinelda Smitt,
  Schmelzman, Skaab Goliath, Swarm of Skeletal Bats, Siegfried, The Thing,
  Vampire Spawn Warrior) were transcribed from the rendered pages 67-73 and are
  repeated verbatim in each entity's `traits_md`.
- The plain `pdftotext` reading order interleaves the two columns on pages 15,
  16, 34 and 52. The blueprint text was authored from a column-aware extraction
  instead, so those passages read correctly here even though the audit's source
  file has them scrambled.
- Handouts that never reach the text layer were transcribed separately:
  Geralf's decoded letter (from media/Geralf_s Letter/Decoded.pdf), the READ /
  PLAY sheet music, and the two loot cards below.

The 6 remaining audit lines — all expected, do not chase
- "wellrespected", "demonworshipping", "everincreasing": `pdftotext` drops the
  line-break hyphen; the blueprint has "well-respected", "demon-worshipping",
  "ever-increasing" as printed.
- "Eckhardt Sheck Magda Botler Flora Mayweather Marcus Ritter Silvia Wintsch
  Captain Levi": page 16's Important NPCs table, read as two separate column
  runs. Rebuilt as a real table in the `Important NPCs of Shadowgrange` lore
  entry, with the pairings taken from the printed page.
- The two Influence Dice lines: page 34's Help/Hindrance table, same problem.
  Rebuilt as a markdown table in the `Defending the Breach` encounter.

Judgment calls (not defects)
- Author typos are carried verbatim per tool/content/README.md rule 0:
  "Maurer Estate" / "Mauer Estate", "Acacynian", "thw town hall", "Visgaurd",
  "impracticle", "apparrently", "indistinguishible", "Euroda", "presense",
  "poltgergeist", "dessicated", "earings", "jewelery", "braclets",
  "Skaabs Goliaths", "same plan", "hiearchy", "suicidie", "Camoflage",
  "nectoric", "Siegfried descent", "he barking" — plus "checkes" and "an the
  two" from the stat-block images. The only edits are extraction artifacts
  (line-break hyphenation).
- Only three SRD-name mismatches were re-pointed: the "flying sword" is
  referenced as the SRD's `Animated Flying Sword`, the shriekgeist as `Specter`
  (the module says "statted as a specter"), and the lumberknot as
  `Shambling Mound` (likewise printed).
- Monster Manual stat blocks the bundled SRD does not carry are NOT
  reproduced and NOT referenced — smoke mephit, twig/vine blight, poltergeist,
  grell, crawling claw, flameskull, thug. They stay as printed prose with the
  MM page cite, and the encounters that use them say so in `dmNotes`.
- Siegfried, Rinelda Smitt, Oglor and Shilgengar are `npc` entities (named
  individuals) rather than `monster`; the ten reusable APPENDIX B creatures are
  `monster`. Shilgengar carries the Demonlord of Ashmouth block by reference.
- 29 `creature-action` entities, one per printed attack, named
  "<Action> (<Creature>)" so they stay unique inside the pack.
- Source inconsistencies flagged in `dmNotes`, carried as printed:
  * Fire Geist "Hit Points 45 (6d6 + 6)" — 6d6+6 averages 27.
  * Skaab Goliath swings "its morningstar" in the siege text; its stat block
    has only a Slam.
  * Siegfried's 2nd round animates "the narstad scrapper (APPENDIX B)"; there
    is no narstad scrapper in APPENDIX B and the room description calls the
    same creature a flesh golem (MM p169).
  * The dining-hall XP line awards for "the barlgura and the five crawling
    claws"; the barlgura is Shilgengar.
  * Rinelda's purse is "90 ep and 70 gp" in Chapter 3's Attacking the Horde and
    "5 ep and 92 gp" in the Necromancer Confrontation. Both are kept.
- `Silver-Inlaid Dagger` is cited as "(APPENDIX A)" but has no APPENDIX A
  entry. Its rules text is transcribed from its loot card in
  media/Magic Items/. `Wolfhunter's Quiver` has a loot card and no placement in
  the text at all; it is carried as an item with that noted.
- `Strionic Resonator` exists as a magic-item because the whole adventure turns
  on it, but the module prints no mechanics, rarity or attunement for it. The
  schema-required rarity is a placeholder and its `dmNotes` says so.
- No `blueprint.json`: the module ships no pregenerated characters, only the
  d8 bond table (in the campaign pages) and the third-party Human Variants
  supplement (bundled as a lore PDF). Nothing was invented to fill the gap.
- `Oglor`'s `species_ref` is Human as a placeholder — his printed block is
  *Small construct* and the bundled SRD has no construct species row; his
  `dmNotes` says so.
