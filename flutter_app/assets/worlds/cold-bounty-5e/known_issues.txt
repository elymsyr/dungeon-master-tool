Cold Bounty — port status (world-blueprint 2.0.0)

Re-ported from the PDF from scratch. `--check` is clean and
`audit_coverage.py` reports 99.7% (1 sentence below threshold, see below).

Fixed in 2.0.0
- Corrupted text in Fat Farlsbag's secrets (stray CJK characters) — gone.
- Hobgoblin Henchmen, Mind-controlled Slaves, Ironmouth Spiders, Crag Yetis and
  The Golem Veined with Platinum are now `monster` entities with the printed
  stat block, not NPC prose. The encounter that referenced a nonexistent
  "Hobgoblin Warrior" now points at the real entities.
- Every stat block value re-read from the PDF: Fat Farlsbag CHA 14 (was 13),
  Hobgoblin STR 13 (was 14), slaves STR 12 / INT 8 / WIS 8 / CHA 8 (were all
  10), I'Zor'zah CR 2 / 450 XP with his Int +4, Wis +2 saves and Arcana +4.
- 11 `creature-action` entities (one per printed attack) + SRD trait refs
  (Martial Advantage, Fire Absorption, Immutable Form, Magic Resistance,
  Magic Weapons) instead of prose-only stat blocks.
- Handout A transcribed from the poster art ("REWARD 5000 coins…"); the old
  entry invented a 1,000 gp reward that appears nowhere in the module.
- Invented content removed: Ott's "escaped slave of I'Zor'zah" backstory, the
  hobgoblins' crossbow stat line, Aldara's and Ott's made-up ability scores,
  the blizzard's "1d4 cold per hour" rule, Kahd's attunement requirement.
- Missing text restored: the DM sidebar about missing Farlsbag, the axe pulling
  its owner north, the slaves-as-combatants rule, "Extending the Adventure",
  the author's note, the pregen roster.
- 8 `scene` entities for the adventure's narrative flow (there were none),
  4 quests, 4 encounters, 7 locations with a parent region.
- Entity links (`@[…](entity:slug/Name)`) across descriptions, beats and notes.
- manifest.json listed media/GURPS GCS Characters/I'Zor'zah-the-Azure.gcs with
  a straight apostrophe; the file on disk uses U+2019, so the installer would
  have skipped it. Path fixed.

Judgment calls (not defects — read before "fixing")
- Author typos are carried verbatim per tool/content/README.md rule 0:
  "is own plan", "occcurred", "mine cards", "int he adventure", "catacylsm",
  "ironouth", "Farlsberg", "woth", "labryinthine", "Davinn-Kahr". The only
  edits are extraction artifacts (line-break hyphenation).
- The pregen pages' font drops one letter from every doubled "ll", so the text
  layer reads "al", "wel", "spels", "Wiliam". Those are restored ("all",
  "well", "spells", "William"). That is why audit_coverage still flags one
  sentence ("…Koi Konraght finaly seek revenge…"): the blueprint says
  "finally". Expected, do not chase it.
- The PDF gives the six pregens only a name, a one-line hook and a backstory —
  no class, level, ability scores or gear. The 2nd-level builds in
  blueprint.json are suggestions and each character's dmNotes says so. The
  PHB personality/ideal/bond/flaw fields were left empty rather than invented.
- Half-Elf is kept as a world species entity because Aldara is "half-elven" and
  the 2024 SRD has no Half-Elf; the entry carries no invented mechanics.
- Aldara, Ott, Count Conrad and Prince Adelheid have no stat blocks, because
  the module prints none.
- I'Zor'zah's sidebar text is cut off mid-sentence in the PDF ("…he has lacked
  the materials and"). Carried as printed, flagged in his dmNotes.
- Mold Earth / Earth Tremor (named in The Finale) are not in the bundled SRD,
  so they are mentioned in text only, not linked as spells.
