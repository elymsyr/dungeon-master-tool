# Messages

One-on-one and group chats.

- **New chat**: one person for a direct chat, several for a group.
- **Group settings**: rename, add or remove members, see the member list.
- **Leave Group**: if you are the admin, the role passes to another member.
- **Delete Group** (admin): deletes all messages for everyone; cannot be undone.

You can message an applicant straight from your [game listing](help:game-listings).
