# Session & Combat

The game table. The [battle map](help:battle-map) fills the screen; the **encounter** (turn order) is on the left with its controls in a bar at the bottom; the **event log** is bottom right.

## Encounters

An encounter is one fight with its own combatant list. Keep several and switch between them.

- **New Encounter**, **Rename**.
- **Add from Codex**: pick monsters or NPCs, with a quantity. Or drag cards in from the sidebar. Three goblins are three combatants with their own HP.
- **Quick Add**: a one-off combatant with a name and stats.
- **Add Players…**: brings in the world's characters.

## Running the fight

- **Roll Initiative**: rolls for everyone and sorts. **Reset Initiative** clears it. You can also drag rows to reorder.
- **Roll HP dice**: monsters get rolled HP instead of the average.
- **Next Turn**: moves to the next combatant; the **round** counter advances on its own.
- **Damage / Heal**: from the list or from the token on the map. Damage to a player character is written back to its sheet.
- **Conditions**: standard or custom, optionally for a number of rounds; they count down by themselves.
- **Clear All**: empties the encounter.

On [your own template](help:template-editor) the columns (HP, AC, initiative…) come from the template's **Combat stats** and **Encounter & map** settings.

## Event log & dice

The log records rolls and turns. Type in the quick-log box to add notes; `@` mentions work.

The **dice button** rolls any expression. In an [online world](help:online-play) players' rolls land in your log.
