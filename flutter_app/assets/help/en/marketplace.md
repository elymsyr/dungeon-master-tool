# Marketplace

A free library of community **worlds**, **packages** and **characters**, plus the **official catalog**.

## Getting content

- **Filter** by content type, language and tag. 18+ content is hidden unless you turn it on.
- **Preview** shows the template and contents before downloading.
- **Get / Download** installs a copy that is yours to edit.
- Browsing and the official catalog need no account; other users' content needs an [account](help:accounts).

## Updates

When the publisher releases a new version you get a prompt with **What's new**:

- **Replace local copy**: take the new version; your own changes are lost.
- **Download as new copy**: keep yours, get the new one next to it.
- **Dismiss this version**, or **Mute updates** for the item.

## Publishing

Open a world's, package's or character's **Settings** on its tab, **Share to Marketplace**, fill in title, description, language, tags and the 18+ flag, **Publish**.

- Each publish is an **immutable snapshot**. To update, publish a new snapshot with a **changelog**; downloaders get the update prompt.
- Content on your own template cannot be published yet.
- Deleting an item that has listings removes them permanently.
