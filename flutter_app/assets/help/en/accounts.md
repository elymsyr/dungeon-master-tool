# Accounts

Almost everything works **without an account**: worlds, characters, templates, packages, maps, combat, the player screen and `.dmtz` transfers.

## An account is needed to

1. **Play online**: take a world online or join one. [Playing online](help:online-play)
2. **Publish** to the marketplace. [Marketplace](help:marketplace)
3. **Download other users' content** from the marketplace.

The [feed](help:feed), [messages](help:messages) and [game listings](help:game-listings) also need you signed in. Browsing the marketplace and installing official catalog content do not.

## Profile

After the first sign-in you pick a **username**; others see it on your posts, listings and published items. Sign out and **Delete Account** are in [Settings](help:settings). Deleting the account removes what is stored online (profile, posts, listings, shared worlds, uploaded media). Your local data stays on the device.

There is no paid tier. Every user has full access.
