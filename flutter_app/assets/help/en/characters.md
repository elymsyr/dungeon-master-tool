# Characters

Every character you own, across all worlds.

## Creating (D&D 5e template)

**New character** runs the creator:

1. **Identity**: name, template, level, portrait.
2. **Species**, **Class**, **Subclass**, **Background**.
3. **Abilities**: standard array, 4d6 drop lowest, or point buy.
4. **Feats**, **Proficiencies & languages**, **Spells**, **Equipment**.
5. **Personality**, **Review**, create.

Options come from the built-in SRD and from the [packages](help:packages) you choose as content sources.

On [your own template](help:templates) there is no creator: you get a blank sheet and the template's **Guide**. [Your own character sheet](help:free-sheet)

## The sheet

- **Level Up**: shows what the new level gives (HP, proficiency bonus, features, spell slots, resource pools) and asks for choices (subclass, ability increase or feat, spells).
- **Short Rest** / **Long Rest**: reset resources and HP.
- Checks, saves and attacks roll from the sheet.

How it works: the sheet is computed from the cards the character points to every time it opens. AC, initiative, proficiencies and resistances come from species, class, feats and equipment cards. Edit one of those cards and the sheet follows. A missing card (removed package) shows as a warning, not an error.

## Characters and worlds

- A character can belong to one world. In a world, **Import Character** adds one that is in no world yet.
- **Release** gives up ownership; the character stays in its world for someone else to claim. **Claim** takes a free one.
- **Copy** duplicates, **Delete** moves it to the Trash.
- **Transfer**: `.dmtz` export / import. [Saving & your data](help:saving)

In an [online world](help:online-play) each player edits only their own characters; the DM sees all of them.
