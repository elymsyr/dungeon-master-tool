# Quick Start

## 1. Create a world

**Worlds → Create**. Name it and pick the **D&D 5e (SRD 5.2.1)** template. Tick **Add D&D 5e SRD content** to get the SRD spells, monsters and items in the world. [Worlds](help:worlds)

## 2. Fill the Codex

Open the world. The **Codex** holds every card. Turn on **Edit mode** in the top bar and add an NPC or a location. Type `@` in a text field to link another card. [Codex](help:codex)

## 3. Make characters

**Characters → New character** runs the step-by-step creator. Put the character in your world, or add it from inside the world with **Import Character**. [Characters](help:characters)

## 4. Prepare the table

- **Session**: load a battle map and add tonight's monsters to an encounter. [Session & combat](help:session) · [Battle map](help:battle-map)
- **Map**: pin locations on the world map. [World map](help:world-map)
- **Mind Map**: sketch plot threads. [Mind map](help:mind-map)

## 5. Play

Roll initiative in **Session** and send the battle map to a second screen. [Player screen](help:projection)

Remote players: take the world online and send them the invite code. [Playing online](help:online-play)

There is no Save button. Every change is saved as you make it. [Saving & your data](help:saving)
