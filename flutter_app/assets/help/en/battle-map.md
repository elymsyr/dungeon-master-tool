# Battle Map

Lives in the [Session](help:session) tab: tokens, fog, drawings and spell areas.

## Setup

- **Open Map**: an image from your device, or one of a location card's battlemaps.
- **Add Map**: place another image on top of or under the current one to stitch maps.
- **Grid**: on/off, **cell size**, **snap to grid**, **feet per cell**, **diagonal rule**.

## Tools

| Tool | Does |
|---|---|
| **Navigate** | Select and move tokens; pan and zoom. |
| **Draw** | Freehand, any color. |
| **Ruler / AoE / shapes** | Measure and mark areas. Layers: *Background*, *Object*, *GM only* (never shown to players). |
| **Erase** | Drag over rulers, areas, shapes, drawings. |
| **Fog** | **Add**, **Erase**, **Fill**, **Clear**; **Reveal** / **Hide** rooms as the party explores. |

## Tokens

Encounter combatants appear as tokens. Drag to move, **resize**, apply **damage** / **heal**. **Clean tokens** hides HP bars and conditions; **Show HP** decides whether players see monster and NPC HP. Free text **labels** can be added.

## Showing it

**Project** sends the map to the [player screen](help:projection). The player view **follows** your pan and zoom; **Lock** it to look around privately. Fog, GM-only shapes and hidden HP never reach players.

In an [online world](help:online-play), a player can move their own token during their own turn. The move goes through the server, which checks that it is that player's turn.
