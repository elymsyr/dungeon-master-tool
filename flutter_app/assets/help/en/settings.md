# Settings

Open from the profile menu in the hub, or **App Settings** in a world's menu.

| Setting | Does |
|---|---|
| **Language** | The app's language. |
| **SRD content language** | Shows SRD cards translated where a translation exists. The data stays English; only the display changes, per device. |
| **Theme** | The look of the app. |
| **Dice theme** | How dice look; *Auto* follows the theme. |
| **Volume** | Overall [Soundpad](help:pdf-soundpad) volume. |
| **Sound Library** | Music **themes** with states, **ambience** and **SFX** files. |
| **Trash** | Restore or permanently delete; auto-delete after 30 days; **Empty Trash**. [Saving & your data](help:saving) |
| **Data** | Where worlds and cache live on disk. |
| **Account** | Profile, sign out, **Delete Account**. [Accounts](help:accounts) |

Found a bug? The bug button in the hub, or **Report a Bug** in a menu, sends a report.
