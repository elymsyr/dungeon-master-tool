# Player Screen

Shows players what you choose and nothing else.

## Outputs

The player screen controls in the world's top bar send to one or more outputs:

- **Pop-out window** (desktop): a separate window; drag it to a second monitor or TV.
- **Screen cast**: a cast target on supported devices.
- **Online players**: everyone connected to an [online world](help:online-play), live.

## What to show

- **Images**: right-click (long-press) an image on a card or map, **Project**.
- **Battle map**: **Project** on the [battle map](help:battle-map). Fog, GM-only shapes and hidden HP stay hidden.
- **World map**. [World map](help:world-map)

## Control

- **Blackout**: the player screen goes dark at once, for example while you set up a reveal.
- **Clear all projections**: removes everything shown.

The pop-out window only draws what the DM window sends it. Close it from the DM side.
