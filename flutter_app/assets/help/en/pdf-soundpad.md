# PDF & Soundpad

## PDF

- **Open File**: the PDF is **copied into the world**, so it travels with it, including in `.dmtz` exports.
- **Library**: the world's PDFs. Several open at once as tabs.
- **Remove**: deletes the file from the world.

On desktop the viewer is a side panel opened from the top bar.

## Soundpad

- **Music**: pick a **theme**, then switch its **state** (*exploration*, *combat*, *victory*…) and **intensity** (*Base*, *Low*, *Medium*, *High*). Tracks crossfade and loop without gaps.
- **Ambience**: layer background sounds (rain, tavern, wind) in separate slots.
- **SFX**: one-shot effects on buttons.
- **Master volume**, **Stop ambience**, **Stop all**.

Build themes and add sound files in **Settings → Sound Library**, or download soundpacks. [Settings](help:settings)
