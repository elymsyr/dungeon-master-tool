# Feed

- **Post**: write in the box at the top; optionally **attach** one of your marketplace items or game listings.
- **Scopes**: **All**, **Following**, **Discover** (new people and content), **Game Lists** (open [game listings](help:game-listings)).
- **Follow** people from their profile or from suggestions in the [marketplace](help:marketplace).

Posts with inappropriate language are rejected; posting too fast is rate-limited.
