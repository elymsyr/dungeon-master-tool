# Social

Where you meet other players and DMs.

- **[Feed](help:feed)**: posts from people you follow, plus discovery.
- **[Messages](help:messages)**: direct and group chats.
- **[Game Listings](help:game-listings)**: find a table or recruit players.
- **[Marketplace](help:marketplace)**: free community worlds, packages and characters, plus the official catalog.

Feed, messages and listings need an [account](help:accounts); the marketplace can be browsed without one. A badge on the Social tab means unread messages or notifications.
