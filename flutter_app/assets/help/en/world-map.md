# World Map & Timeline

The **Map** tab: a map image covered in pins that can change over time.

## Basics

- **Load Map**: pick the image.
- **Right-click** (long-press) to place a pin. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
- **Add from DB**: pin an existing [Codex](help:codex) card, such as a city.
- Click a pin to open its card. A **location** card can carry its own map, so you can drill from the world into a city, then a dungeon. **Home** returns to the top.
- **Pin categories**: show or hide pins by category. **Hide All** clears the view.

## Timeline & eras

- **Era** waypoints split history into steps (*Year 1012*, *After the war*). Each waypoint can have its own map image and pins.
- **Copy to…** copies pins to another waypoint.
- **Timeline pins** mark events on a given **day** and can be linked to a session.

The map can be [projected](help:projection) like any image.
