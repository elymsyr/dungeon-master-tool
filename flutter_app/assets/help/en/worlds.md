# Worlds

A **world** is one campaign: its cards, maps, mind maps, encounters, PDFs and sounds, on top of one [template](help:templates). [How it fits together](help:concepts)

## Worlds tab

- **Create**: name and template. **Add D&D 5e SRD content** links the SRD package into the world.
- **Load World**: opens the selected world.
- **Join**: enter a DM's invite code. [Playing online](help:online-play)
- **Settings** (on a world card): name, cover, description, multiplayer and invites, **Share to Marketplace**.
- **Transfer**: `.dmtz` export / import. [Saving & your data](help:saving)
- **Delete**: moves the world to the Trash. An online world's cloud copy is removed too.
- **Filter**: by template or package.

Badges: **Online · DM**, **Online · Player**.

A world on **your own template** stays on this device for now: it cannot go multiplayer or to the marketplace, because players would get the built-in sheet instead of yours.

## Inside a world

| Tab | For |
|---|---|
| **Codex** | All cards. [Codex](help:codex) |
| **Session** | Encounters, battle map, event log. [Session & combat](help:session) |
| **Mind Map** | Planning board. [Mind map](help:mind-map) |
| **Map** | World map, pins, timeline. [World map](help:world-map) |
| **PDF** / **Soundpad** | Rulebooks and table audio. [PDF & Soundpad](help:pdf-soundpad) |
| **Characters** | The world's characters. [Characters](help:characters) |

On desktop, PDF, Soundpad and Characters open as side panels.

**Top bar:** back to hub · Undo/Redo · save indicator · help · **Edit / View mode** · player screen controls.

- **View mode** is for the table: cards are read-only and clean.
- **Edit mode** lets you change cards, fields and layout.

If the world is on your own template and that template was edited since the world last opened, the world offers the update: **Update**, **Ignore**, or never ask again. Updating never touches card values; data of a removed field stays hidden on the card and comes back if the field returns.
