# Playing Online

Remote players join a world with a code and see only what you share. Needs an [account](help:accounts).

## As the DM

1. Worlds tab → world **Settings** → **Multiplayer On**. The world and its media upload to the cloud.
2. Send players the 8-character **invite code**. **Regenerate** it if it leaks; the old code stops working.
3. **Accept** or **Reject** join requests.
4. [Share cards](help:codex) with all or chosen players. **Share a package** to give everyone its content.
5. [Project](help:projection) images and the battle map to everyone connected.

**Multiplayer Off** removes the world and its member data from the cloud. The local copy stays.

Worlds on your own template cannot go multiplayer yet.

## As a player

1. Worlds tab → **Join**, enter the code.
2. Wait for approval. The world appears with an **Online · Player** badge.
3. You see the cards shared with you, your own [character](help:characters) and the player screen.
4. On your turn you can move your token. Your dice rolls go to the DM's log.

**Leave** removes the world from your device; characters you owned there become free to claim.

## How it works

- Your edits go to the cloud a few seconds after you make them. The [save indicator](help:saving) shows when everything is in sync.
- Players receive only: what you project, cards you share (without DM notes or DM-only fields), world characters, shared packages and the member list. Everything else never leaves your device.
- A player keeps shared cards in their own app and downloads only new or changed ones on reconnect.
- Turn moves and dice rolls are the only things players send back; the server checks a move is made on that player's turn.

**Media limits:** maps up to 10 MB, other images 5 MB, audio 10 MB. Larger files stay local and do not reach players. Cloud storage has a quota.
